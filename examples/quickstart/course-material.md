# Authorized course material

## Cost behavior

Fixed costs do not change with the number of units produced or sold within the relevant range. Examples can include rent for a business location or a fixed monthly software subscription.

Variable costs change with output. If each unit requires €12 of materials, total material cost increases by €12 for each additional unit.

## Contribution margin

Contribution margin per unit is:

**selling price per unit − variable cost per unit**

The contribution margin indicates how much each unit contributes toward covering fixed costs and, after fixed costs are covered, toward profit.

## Break-even quantity

A simple break-even quantity can be calculated as:

**fixed costs ÷ contribution margin per unit**

Example: if fixed costs are €2,000, selling price is €50 per unit, and variable cost is €30 per unit, contribution margin is €20 per unit and break-even quantity is 100 units.
